About

Pittsburgh kid. Denison grad. Happiest on a set with a long day ahead.

I grew up in Moon Township, just outside Pittsburgh, and graduated from Denison University in May 2026 with a B.A. in Cinema and a minor in Music. Most of my degree was hands-on: three levels of production classes, a documentary, a short I wrote and produced, and crew roles on more student shoots than I can count.

Outside of film I've been on stage since I was a kid, which is where I learned to take direction, adapt fast, and keep going when something breaks. I like coordinating across departments, operating equipment, and keeping a shoot on schedule. Long hours don't bother me.

I'm looking for work in Pittsburgh's film, video, and theater world: editing, production, or anywhere a crew needs someone who shows up ready.
